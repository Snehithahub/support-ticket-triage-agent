# Managing team members and roles

This article is for account Owners and Admins who need to invite team members, assign roles, or change the account Owner or registered contact details.

## Team roles

| Role | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Owner | One per account. Full control, including API keys and team management. |
| Admin | Manages team members, settings, and API keys. |
| Finance | Settlements, reports, and invoices. |
| Developer | API integration and webhooks. |
| Support | Looking up payments and helping customers. |
| Viewer | Read-only access. |

Only Owner and Admin users can invite members and view or regenerate API keys. Both roles must use two-factor authentication (2FA).

## Inviting a team member

1. Log in to the Dashboard as an Owner or Admin.
2. Open team management in the Dashboard settings.
3. Click Invite member.
4. Enter the person's work email address and choose a role.
5. Send the invite. The person sets up their own password when they accept.

Give each person the least access they need, and give everyone their own login rather than sharing one. Remove users promptly when they leave your business. If a team member is locked out or loses their 2FA device, the Owner can unlock them or reset their 2FA.

## Changing the account Owner

Each account has exactly one Owner. To transfer ownership, for example when a founder leaves or the business is sold, send a signed request from the business to support@northwindpay.example. The request should name the current Owner and the new Owner. If the ownership of the business itself has changed, re-KYC is also required. See KYC-04.

## Changing the registered email or phone

To change the registered email address or phone number, Northwind Pay verifies both the old and the new contact. If you no longer have access to the old email or phone, you need to complete identity verification instead. Support never asks for your password or OTPs during this process.

## Still need help?

Contact support@northwindpay.example from the registered email address with your business name, the user involved, and the change you need. See also: ACC-01, ACC-02, ACC-03.

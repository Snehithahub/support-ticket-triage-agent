# Changing your settlement bank account

This article is for merchants who need to change the bank account their settlements are paid into. It explains the documents you need, how long verification takes, and what happens to your settlements in the meantime.

## Before you start

Changing your settlement bank account is a business detail change, so plan it carefully:

- Make sure the new account is in the same legal name as your business. A name mismatch between your PAN and bank account is a common reason for rejection.
- Avoid making the change just before an expected large settlement, because settlements pause during verification.
- Only users with the right permissions on your account can request the change.

## Documents you need

Upload one of the following for the new account:

- A cancelled cheque showing the account holder name, account number, and IFSC.
- A recent bank statement showing the same details.

Make sure the document is clear and not blurred or cropped, so that it can be verified on the first attempt.

## How to change the account

1. Log in to dashboard.northwindpay.example.
2. Go to **Settings > Business Details** and open the bank account section.
3. Enter the new account details.
4. Upload the cancelled cheque or bank statement.
5. Submit the request.

You will see the status of the new account in the same section while it is being verified.

## What happens during verification

- Re-verification takes 1-2 business days.
- Settlements pause until the new account is verified. Payments you capture during this time are not lost; they are settled once verification is complete.
- If verification fails, your settlements stay on hold. Upload a corrected document to try again.

A change of bank account can also trigger a re-KYC request, since bank account changes are one of the business details that require re-verification. See KYC-04.

## Still need help?

Email support@northwindpay.example with your account details, the date you submitted the change, and any rejection message shown in the Dashboard. Never send full bank login details or passwords. See also: SET-02, KYC-04, KYC-02.

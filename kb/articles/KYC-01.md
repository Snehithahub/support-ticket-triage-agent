# Documents required for KYC

This article is for merchants setting up a new Northwind Pay account who need to complete KYC (Know Your Customer) verification. It lists the documents you must submit and how to prepare them so they are accepted the first time.

## Why KYC is required

KYC confirms that your business is genuine and that settlements go to the right bank account. Until your KYC is verified, you can accept payments only up to a limited total and your settlements stay on hold. Live API keys are also available only after KYC is verified. See KYC-03 for limits and timelines.

## Required documents

Submit all of the following:

- **PAN:** The PAN of the business. Sole proprietors submit the proprietor's PAN.
- **GSTIN certificate:** Required if your business is registered for GST.
- **Bank account proof:** A cancelled cheque or a bank statement for the account you want settlements paid into.
- **Business proof:** One of the following, depending on your business type:
  - Certificate of incorporation (companies)
  - Partnership deed (partnerships)
  - Shop establishment certificate (proprietorships and small businesses)
- **Address proof of the authorized signatory:** A valid address proof for the person authorized to act for the business.

## Preparing your documents

Most rejections come from document quality or mismatched details. Before uploading:

1. Scan or photograph each document in full, with all four edges visible.
2. Make sure the text is sharp and readable, not blurred.
3. Check that no document has expired.
4. Confirm that the name on your PAN matches the name on your bank account.
5. Make sure your website shows a refund policy, terms and conditions, and contact details, because these are checked as part of verification.

## How to submit

1. Log in to dashboard.northwindpay.example.
2. Open the KYC or account activation section.
3. Upload each document in the matching field.
4. Review your business details and submit.

Verification starts once all documents are submitted, and usually takes 2-3 business days. If anything is missing, the Dashboard will show which document is still needed.

## Still need help?

Contact support@northwindpay.example with your registered business name and the document you are unsure about. Do not email copies of documents unless support asks for them. See also: KYC-02, KYC-03, KYC-04.

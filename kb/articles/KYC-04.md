# Re-KYC and updating business details

This article is for merchants who have been asked to complete re-KYC, or who need to update their business details. It explains when re-KYC is required and how to complete it without interrupting your settlements.

## When re-KYC is required

Northwind Pay asks you to complete KYC again:

- **Every 2 years**, as a periodic review of your account.
- **When key business details change**, including:
  - Business name
  - PAN
  - Ownership
  - Settlement bank account

You will be notified in the Dashboard and by email when re-KYC is due.

## Updating your business details

1. Log in to dashboard.northwindpay.example.
2. Go to **Settings > Business Details**.
3. Edit the detail that has changed.
4. Upload the supporting documents for the change. For example, a new bank account needs a cancelled cheque or bank statement, and a change of name or PAN needs the updated PAN and business proof.
5. Submit the update for verification.

Make sure the new details match across all documents. A name mismatch between your PAN and bank account is a common reason for rejection.

## Documents for re-KYC

Re-KYC uses the same document list as your original KYC:

- PAN of the business, or proprietor PAN
- GSTIN certificate, if registered
- Bank account proof (cancelled cheque or bank statement)
- Business proof (certificate of incorporation, partnership deed, or shop establishment certificate)
- Address proof of the authorized signatory

Check that none of your previously submitted documents have expired. See KYC-01 for details.

## Effect on your account

- Changing your settlement bank account pauses settlements until the new account is verified, which takes 1-2 business days. See SET-05.
- If re-KYC is not completed, or documents are rejected, settlements may be placed on hold until verification is complete.
- Changing the account Owner is handled separately and requires a signed request from the business.

To avoid disruption, complete re-KYC as soon as you are notified, and plan bank account changes away from expected large settlements.

## Still need help?

Email support@northwindpay.example with your registered business name, the detail you are changing, and any rejection reason shown in the Dashboard. See also: KYC-01, KYC-02, SET-05.

# Why was my KYC rejected?

This article is for merchants whose KYC submission was rejected. It explains the most common rejection reasons, how to fix each one, and how to resubmit.

## Find the rejection reason

When KYC is rejected, the Dashboard shows the reason next to the affected document or detail. You may also receive an email. Read the reason carefully before resubmitting, because uploading the same document again will usually be rejected again.

## Common rejection reasons and fixes

| Reason | How to fix it |
|---|---|
| Blurred or cropped document | Upload a clear scan or photo showing the full document with all edges visible. |
| Name mismatch between PAN and bank account | Use a bank account held in the same name as your PAN, or update the account so the names match. |
| Expired document | Upload a current, valid version of the document. |
| Website missing refund policy, terms, or contact details | Add a refund policy, terms and conditions, and contact details to your website, then resubmit. |
| Prohibited business category | Northwind Pay cannot onboard businesses in prohibited categories. Contact support if you believe your category was classified incorrectly. |

## Name mismatch in detail

A name mismatch is one of the most frequent reasons. The legal name on your business PAN, or the proprietor's PAN for sole proprietors, must match the account holder name on your bank account proof. Small differences, such as a missing word in the business name, can cause a mismatch. If your bank records use a different name, ask your bank to correct them, or use another account that matches.

## How to resubmit

1. Log in to dashboard.northwindpay.example.
2. Open the KYC section and find the rejected item.
3. Fix the issue described in the rejection reason.
4. Upload the corrected document or update the detail.
5. Submit again.

Verification takes 2-3 business days after all documents are submitted. While you wait, your account can accept payments up to Rs 50,000 in total, but settlements stay on hold. See KYC-03.

### Tips to avoid another rejection

- Check every document, not only the rejected one, for expiry and clarity.
- Make sure the details you typed in the Dashboard match your documents exactly.
- Review your website's policy pages before resubmitting.

## Still need help?

Email support@northwindpay.example with your registered business name and the exact rejection reason shown in the Dashboard. See also: KYC-01, KYC-03, SET-05.

# Changing your plan or requesting custom pricing

This article explains how to move to a different Northwind Pay plan or ask for custom pricing, and when the new rates take effect.

## Which plan fits your business

- **Standard**: no setup fee and no annual maintenance fee; 2% per transaction on domestic payments, 3% on international cards, Diners, and Amex, and 2.5% on EMI.
- **Growth**: for businesses processing over Rs 50 lakh per month, with a discounted rate of 1.75% on domestic payments.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing for businesses processing over Rs 5 crore per month.

GST at 18% applies to fees on every plan. See PRC-01 for the full breakdown.

## How to request a change

You can request a plan change in either of two ways:

1. **From the Dashboard**: go to Settings > Plan, review your current plan, and submit a request for the plan you want.
2. **By email**: write to sales@northwindpay.example.

The Growth plan and Enterprise pricing both require a request to the sales team; they are not applied automatically when your volume grows.

To help the sales team review your request quickly, include:

- Your business name and registered email address
- Your average monthly processing volume
- Your main payment methods, such as cards, UPI, or international payments
- Any specific needs, such as international payments or Instant Settlements

## When new pricing takes effect

Pricing changes take effect from the 1st of the next month. Payments captured before then are charged at your current rates, even if the change is approved earlier in the month.

Your new rates appear in the fees deducted from your settlements and on your monthly GST tax invoice, which is generated on the 3rd for the previous calendar month. Check the first invoice after the change to confirm the new rates. If a fee was charged at the wrong rate, support can issue a credit note on your next invoice.

## Still need help?

Email sales@northwindpay.example for plan or pricing requests, or support@northwindpay.example with the invoice month and settlement IDs (`setl_...`) if your rates look wrong after a change. See also: PRC-01, PRC-02, INV-03.

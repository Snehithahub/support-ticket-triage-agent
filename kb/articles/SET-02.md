# Why is my settlement delayed or on hold?

This article is for merchants whose settlement has not reached their bank account on the expected date. It explains the difference between a normal delay and a hold, the reasons a hold is placed, and how to resolve each one.

## First, check for a normal delay

Before assuming a hold, confirm the expected date:

- Domestic payments settle T+2 business days after capture.
- International payments settle T+7 business days after capture.
- Settlements are not processed on bank holidays or Sundays and move to the next business day.

If the expected date has not passed yet once holidays are counted, the settlement is not delayed. See SET-01.

## Reasons a settlement is put on hold

Northwind Pay places settlements on hold for these reasons:

- **Pending KYC.** Until your KYC is verified, settlements stay on hold. See KYC-03.
- **Risk review.** An unusual spike in payment volume or in refunds can trigger a review of your account.
- **Bank account verification failure.** If your settlement bank account could not be verified, or you recently changed it, settlements pause until it is verified. See SET-05.
- **Open disputes above Rs 1,00,000.** If the total of your open disputes is above Rs 1,00,000, settlements are held.

The Dashboard shows a notice on the settlement when it is on hold. You may also receive an email with the reason.

## Resolving each hold

1. **Pending KYC:** Submit any missing documents. Verification takes 2-3 business days after all documents are submitted.
2. **Risk review:** Reply promptly to any request for information, such as invoices or an explanation of the volume increase. Risk reviews usually complete in 3-5 business days.
3. **Bank account verification:** Upload a clear cancelled cheque or bank statement that matches your business name. Re-verification takes 1-2 business days.
4. **Open disputes:** Respond to open disputes with evidence so they can be resolved. Accepted or resolved disputes reduce the open total.

## Settlement lower than expected

If a settlement arrived but the amount is smaller than you expected, it is not on hold. The difference usually comes from refunds, fees, GST on fees, or dispute deductions. Use the settlement report to check. See SET-03.

# Northwind Pay pricing plans

This article summarizes the Standard, Growth, and Enterprise plans so you can see what you pay and which plan fits your business.

## Standard plan

The Standard plan has no setup fee and no annual maintenance fee. You pay only per transaction:

| Payment method | Fee per transaction |
|---|---|
| Domestic cards, netbanking, wallets, and UPI (including UPI on RuPay credit cards) | 2% |
| EMI | 2.5% |
| Diners and Amex | 3% |
| International cards and international payment methods | 3% |

GST at 18% applies on all fees. International payments must be enabled separately before you can accept them. See PRC-02.

## Growth plan

The Growth plan is for businesses processing over Rs 50 lakh per month. It offers a discounted rate of 1.75% on domestic payments. The Growth plan is not applied automatically; you need to send a request to the sales team.

## Enterprise plan

Enterprise offers custom pricing for businesses processing over Rs 5 crore per month. Rates are agreed with the sales team based on your business's volume and needs.

## Other charges

These charges apply in addition to transaction fees, each with GST at 18%:

- **Normal refunds**: no refund fee, but the original transaction fee is not returned.
- **Instant refunds**: 0.5% of the refund amount, with a minimum of Rs 5.
- **Instant Settlements**: 0.25% of the settled amount.
- **Chargebacks**: Rs 500 per chargeback, charged only if the dispute is lost.
- **Currency conversion**: a 1% markup on top of fees for non-INR settlements.

All fees are deducted from your settlements, and a GST tax invoice listing them is generated on the 3rd of each month. Pricing changes take effect from the 1st of the next month. To change plans, see PRC-03.

## Still need help?

For Growth or Enterprise pricing, email sales@northwindpay.example with your business name and monthly processing volume. For a fee question on a specific payment, contact support@northwindpay.example with the payment ID (`pay_...`). See also: PRC-02, PRC-03, INV-03.

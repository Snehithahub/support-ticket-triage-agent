# International payments pricing and enablement

This article explains how to start accepting payments from international cards and payment methods, what they cost, and how they settle.

## Enabling international payments

International payments are not available by default. They must be enabled separately, and your account must meet these requirements:

- Your KYC is verified.
- Your website is live and shows your policies, including a refund and cancellation policy, terms and conditions, and contact details.

To request enablement:

1. Confirm in the Dashboard that your KYC is verified.
2. Check that your website displays the required policies and contact details.
3. Contact support@northwindpay.example to request international payments for your account.
4. Once enabled, make a test payment and check that it appears correctly in the Dashboard.

## Pricing

| Charge | Rate |
|---|---|
| International cards and international payment methods | 3% per transaction |
| Currency conversion markup (non-INR settlements) | 1% on top of fees |
| GST | 18% on fees |

Diners and Amex cards are also charged at 3% per transaction.

## Settlement timelines

International payments settle on a T+7 business day cycle, where T is the capture date. Domestic payments settle on T+2 business days, so expect international funds to reach your bank later. Settlements are not processed on Sundays or bank holidays and move to the next business day.

## Before you go live

- Show prices clearly at checkout, including the currency.
- Make sure your refund policy is visible at checkout. International customers can raise disputes just like domestic ones, and you must respond within 7 calendar days.
- Refunds follow the same rules as domestic payments: they can be issued within 180 days of capture, and the original transaction fee is not returned.

## Still need help?

Contact support@northwindpay.example with your business name, website URL, and, for a specific payment, the payment ID (`pay_...`). See also: PRC-01, PRC-03, SET-01.

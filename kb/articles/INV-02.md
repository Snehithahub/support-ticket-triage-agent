# Correcting GST and business details on invoices

This article explains how to fix your GSTIN, legal name, or billing address on Northwind Pay tax invoices, and how credit notes work when a fee was charged incorrectly.

## Update details for future invoices

Your invoice details come from Settings > Business Details in the Dashboard.

1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2. Go to Settings > Business Details.
3. Update your GSTIN, legal name, or billing address.
4. Save your changes.

Invoices are generated on the 3rd of each month for the previous calendar month. Make your changes before the 3rd so they appear on the next invoice. Changes apply only to future invoices; they do not update invoices already generated.

Make sure your legal name matches your GST registration exactly. Changing your business name, PAN, ownership, or bank account may also trigger re-KYC. See KYC-04.

## Re-issue a past invoice

If a recent invoice has wrong details, you can ask support to re-issue it. Re-issue is available only for invoices from the previous 2 months. Older invoices cannot be re-issued.

To request a re-issue:

1. Correct the details in Settings > Business Details first.
2. Email support@northwindpay.example with the invoice month or months, the field that was wrong, and the correct value.
3. Download the re-issued invoice from Reports > Invoices once support confirms it is ready.

## Credit notes for incorrect fees

If you believe a fee was charged incorrectly, for example a rate that does not match your plan or a charge that appears twice, contact support with the details. If the fee is confirmed as incorrect, Northwind Pay issues a credit note. The credit note appears on your next invoice rather than as a change to the old one.

Before raising a fee query, compare the fee lines on your invoice with your settlement reports in Reports > Settlements. See INV-03.

## Still need help?

Contact support@northwindpay.example with your business name, GSTIN, the invoice month, and any related settlement IDs (`setl_...`). See also: INV-01, INV-03, KYC-04.

# Downloading your monthly tax invoice

This article explains when your Northwind Pay GST tax invoice is generated, how to download it, and what it contains.

## When invoices are generated

Northwind Pay generates one GST tax invoice per month for the fees charged on your account. The invoice is generated on the 3rd of each month and covers the previous calendar month. For example, the invoice for fees charged in September is generated on 3 October.

The invoice is a statement of fees that have already been deducted from your settlements. You do not need to make a separate payment against it.

## How to download your invoice

1. Log in to the Dashboard at dashboard.northwindpay.example.
2. Go to Reports > Invoices.
3. Find the month you need.
4. Click Download to save the invoice as a PDF.

If the invoice for the month that just ended is not listed yet, check the date. It appears on the 3rd.

## What the invoice contains

- Your business's legal name, GSTIN, and billing address, as set in Settings > Business Details
- Total transaction fees for the month
- Other charges, such as instant refund fees, Instant Settlement fees, and chargeback fees
- GST at 18% on all fees
- Any credit notes for fees that were charged incorrectly

Use the invoice for your GST filings and to reconcile fees against your settlement reports in Reports > Settlements.

## If something on the invoice is wrong

If your GSTIN, legal name, or billing address is incorrect, update it in Settings > Business Details before the 3rd so the next invoice is correct. Changes apply only to future invoices. Past invoices can be re-issued for the previous 2 months on request. See INV-02.

If a fee looks incorrect, contact support. If the fee was charged in error, a credit note is issued and appears on your next invoice.

## Still need help?

Email support@northwindpay.example with your business name, GSTIN, and the invoice month. For fee queries, include the related settlement IDs (`setl_...`). See also: INV-02, INV-03, SET-03.

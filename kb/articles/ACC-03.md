# Account locked after failed login attempts

This article explains why your Dashboard login may be locked and how to get back in.

## Why accounts get locked

After 5 failed login attempts, Northwind Pay locks the account for 30 minutes. This protects you from someone trying to guess your password.

Common causes include:

- A mistyped or outdated password, often one saved in the browser
- A password manager filling in credentials for a different account
- Caps Lock or a changed keyboard layout
- Several people sharing one login, which you should avoid

## How to unlock

You have three options:

1. **Wait 30 minutes.** The lock is removed automatically, and you can log in with the correct password.
2. **Ask your account Owner.** If you are a team member, the Owner can unlock your user from the Dashboard so you do not have to wait.
3. **Reset your password.** If you are not sure of your password, use Forgot password on the login page rather than guessing again. Reset links expire in 30 minutes. See ACC-01.

If you are the account Owner, wait for the automatic unlock or reset your password.

## After you get back in

- Update any saved passwords in your browser or password manager.
- Make sure each team member has their own user. Only the Owner and Admins can invite members. See ACC-04.
- Check that two-factor authentication (2FA) is turned on. It is mandatory for Owners and Admins.

## If you did not cause the lock

If you did not make the failed attempts, someone else may be trying to access your account. Once you are back in, change your password right away and confirm that 2FA is turned on. Northwind Pay support never asks for your password or OTPs, so treat any such request as a scam. Report suspicious activity to support@northwindpay.example.

## Still need help?

Contact support@northwindpay.example from your registered email address with your business name, your Dashboard user email, and the approximate time the lock happened. See also: ACC-01, ACC-02, ACC-04.

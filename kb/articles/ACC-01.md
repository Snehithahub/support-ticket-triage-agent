# Resetting your password

This article explains how to reset your Northwind Pay Dashboard password if you have forgotten it, and what to do if the reset email does not arrive.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. Go to the login page at dashboard.northwindpay.example.
2. Click Forgot password.
3. Enter the email address registered to your Dashboard user.
4. Open the reset email and click the link.
5. Set a new password and log in.

Reset links expire 30 minutes after they are sent. If your link has expired, request a new one from the login page. If you requested more than one link, use the most recent email.

If two-factor authentication (2FA) is turned on for your user, you still need your authenticator app, SMS OTP, or a backup code to log in after resetting your password.

## If the reset email does not arrive

- Check your spam, junk, and promotions folders.
- Confirm you entered the email address registered to your Dashboard user. If you are unsure which address your user was invited with, ask your account Owner or an Admin.
- Ask your IT team whether your company's mail server is filtering messages from external senders.
- Wait a few minutes before requesting another link.

If you no longer have access to the registered email, it needs to be changed. This requires verification of both the old and new contact, or identity verification if the old one is unavailable. See ACC-04.

## Choosing a strong password

- Use a long, unique password that you do not use on any other site.
- Consider a password manager to generate and store it.
- Do not share your password with colleagues. Ask an Owner or Admin to invite them as users with their own login instead.

## Keeping your account secure

Northwind Pay support never asks for your password or OTPs, by email, phone, or chat. If someone asks for them, do not share them, and report it to support@northwindpay.example.

If you enter the wrong password 5 times, your account is locked for 30 minutes. See ACC-03.

## Still need help?

Contact support@northwindpay.example from your registered email address and include your business name and your Dashboard user email. See also: ACC-02, ACC-03, ACC-04.
